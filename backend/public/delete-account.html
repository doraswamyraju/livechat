Account & Data Deletion Request

App: LetsTrack - Live Chat & CRM | Developer: Rajugari Ventures

At LetsTrack, we respect your right to control your personal data. If you wish to permanently delete your agent account, organization data, or any associated personal information, please follow the steps outlined below.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can request full deletion of your account and all associated data at any time through the following methods:

Submit a Direct Deletion Request

Send an email from your registered LetsTrack account email address with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Send Deletion Request Email
Direct Email: support@letstrack.manacity.in or doraswamyraju1@gmail.com

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Account Profile: Your name, email address, password hash, role, and avatar.
  • Workspace & CRM Data: Customer contact cards, lead records, and custom tags associated with your tenant.
  • Chat History & Logs: Real-time chat messages, visitor session transcripts, and IP/geolocation metadata.
  • Device Push Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device identifiers linked to your session.

Data Retention & Processing Timeline

  • Verification: Our support team will verify ownership of the account within 24–48 hours.
  • Permanent Deletion: All active database records are wiped within 7 business days of verification.
  • Backups: Residual data in encrypted routine disaster recovery backups will be completely overwritten within 30 days.

Questions & Privacy Inquiries

For more details on our data handling practices, please review our Privacy Policy. If you have any questions regarding your data privacy rights, please contact us at support@letstrack.manacity.in.
